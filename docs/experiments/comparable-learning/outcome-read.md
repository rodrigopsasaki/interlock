## Review pair

### Supported observations and cases

- Review-a correctly reports explicit-range quotation checking, revision-range
  refusal, rooted-discovery conversion at hypothesis standing, and the limit

  left no source or test change.
- Review-b correctly reports explicit-range checking, the command branch's
  immediate rooted mark, rooted-discovery hypothesis conversion, the
  non-semantic quotation limit, and the runner fixture's exclusion of an
  invalid explicit-line discovery. The present source check supports those
  descriptions.
- Both reports overlap on range-bounded quotation, the distinction between a
  rooted location and causal support, and filtering an invalid discovery before
  reusable evidence. Overlap is compatible with two bounded reviews and has no
  automatic penalty.
- The received final-line hypothesis was checked and adopted as an existing
  coverage control by review-b's debrief. Review-a had no received hypothesis.
  The six returned absences were coverage observations, not adopted source
  discoveries.

### Materially missed boundary and unsupported assertion

Both reviews omit the sentinel scope distinction: `command` and
`out-of-band citation` become repository-scoped discovery evidence. The
bounded up-to-four-observation ask makes that an omission to retain, not a
required repair or a failed review.

Review-b calls command-form rooting a defect. The source does root command-form
text without command output or exit-status checking, but the supplied
assessment identifies it as deliberate, tested legacy behavior and the report
does not show a violated contract or harmful downstream treatment. “Defect” is
therefore unsupported by the recorded argument. It can remain a design concern;
it is not evidence that the treatment was worse or better. Review-a's
no-defect conclusion remains bounded to its inspected source and tests.

## Narrowest update and next question

The narrowest update is that retained context can be traced through acknowledged
delivery, a frozen database, an identical context response, a committed brief,
and a source-checked debrief use; that trace alone does not establish
report-level use in every case, improved work, or causal benefit. Equal and
qualified outcomes remain evidence.

The next unanswered question is whether a cleanly isolated later pair can yield
a predeclared, source-checked report-level outcome—checked use, justified
rejection, or no use—while an assessment keeps conditions hidden enough to
distinguish that outcome from ordinary source review. That would be a next
check, not a change to runtime, gates, plans, trust settings, or strategy.
